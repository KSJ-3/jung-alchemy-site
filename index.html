The 4 Stages of Jungian Alchemy

In Carl Jung's psychological framework, alchemy was not about turning lead into literal gold, but rather a symbolic map for individuation—the process of integrating the conscious and unconscious self. The four main stages are Nigredo (decomposition/facing the shadow), Albedo (purification and clarity), Citrinitas (wisdom and integration), and Rubedo (wholeness and the Great Work).
